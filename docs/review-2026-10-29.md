# Mockup review: merged findings from 8 reviewers

Reviewers agreed on almost every point. Items marked (n) were raised by n reviewers. Sizes: S is under 1 hour of agent work, M is a few hours, L is more.

## A. Contradicts the current specification or a hard rule (must fix)

**A1. No multiple comparison correction (8)**
- **What and where:** every sentence, dot, badge, ERP triangle, coherence line, slider and summary entry uses uncorrected |z| >= 2. Affected: `textlib.py` THRESHOLD, over() and L-CHANCE-1; `b_results.js` isOut() and the slider; `figures.py`; the PDF; M6, M7 and M13.
- **Spec source:** 3.2 Multiple comparison correction (Working); G2.
- **Proposal:** add a per-value `marked` field, set by hand and declared as new assumption M33 (the method stays open). Use three states: marked, beyond 2 z but unmarked, within 2 z. Replace L-CHANCE-1 with L-MC-1. Sentences, SUM-RULE-1 and the comparison list follow `marked`. Keep the continuous colours. Replace the slider with "marked / all pairs". **L**

**A2. Synthetic z values too narrow (2)**
- **What and where:** the SD of z is 0.4 to 0.6, so only 12 of 2,635 values reach 2 z where about 130 are expected by chance. No value survives a false discovery rate check, so A1 would show nothing. `synth.py`.
- **Spec source:** 3.2; hard rule 1.
- **Proposal:** give the per-recording noise unit variance and keep it smooth across the scalp. Add 3 to 6 stable deviations of 3.5 to 4.5 z spread across blocks. **M**

**A3. Demonstration profile reads as the target condition (2)**
- **What and where:** frontal theta, a raised theta/beta ratio, a lower NoGo P3 and high reaction time variability form the textbook pattern. `synth.py` PROFILES m2.
- **Spec source:** 3.2 No ADHD wording; hard rule 5.
- **Proposal:** choose scattered, condition-neutral deviations together with A2, and update M1. **S**

**A4. Stale question and section numbers (7)**
- **What and where:**
  - Questionnaires appear as "Open question Q4" but are a Later module.
  - Q5 should be Q6, Q11 should be Q8, and Q10 is now the 3.2 working decision.
  - "3.5 Summary rule" and "3.5 Ratios" belong to 3.2.
  - 3.1 is labelled "proposed" but is Decided.
  - The PDF body prints "to confirm (Q5)" in EN, DE and JA.
  - Files: `b_results.js`, `i18n.py`, `synth.py`, README M13 and M19, the digest, the v1 page.
- **Spec source:** section 4; table 3.3.
- **Proposal:** renumber everything. Questionnaires get the later-module text "until certified, clinics use questionnaires in V3". Keep question numbers out of the printed report. **S**

**A5. Template coverage headline is wrong (4)**
- **What and where:** 122 questionnaire elements are classed "open", and the text plans for Q4 options that no longer exist. `coverage/models`, `template-coverage.md`.
- **Spec source:** table 3.3.
- **Proposal:** reclassify those elements as later_module and rerun the counts (about 241 later module, about 43 open). Delete the "Q4 (b)" text, make the anamnesis prefill loss a firm statement, and rebuild the PDF. **M**

**A6. Summary completeness claim is false (1)**
- **What and where:** SUM-RULE-1 says "every measure", but `all_deviations()` leaves out the 1 Hz maps (one value is z = 3.2) and the spectrum bins (one is 3.0).
- **Spec source:** 3.2 No interpretation; table 3.3.
- **Proposal:** list the families explicitly. Global coherence may stay out while it is "to confirm", but SUM-RULE-1 must say so. Show the family counts. **S**

**A7. Clinic users edit and save templates (4)**
- **What and where:** Clinician 1 saved new versions of CT-C-EN and of the company standard. `c_report.js` save().
- **Spec source:** 3.5 Clinic templates (Working); M31.
- **Proposal:** the clinic view is read-only with a "Request a change" action. Editing happens under the company staff actor. A "Release 1 / later" switch shows both states. **M**

**A8. Report contradicts the clinician's choices (1)**
- **What and where:** a marker moved to 9.4 Hz, but the ratios block and APF-IN-1 still say 8.9 Hz. An included segment gives 168 s on screen and 164 s in the report and Q-DUR-1.
- **Spec source:** 3.4 Clinician in control; 3.8 Reproducibility.
- **Proposal:** drive the report values from state. Word each choice as "suggested X, moved to Y" or "confirmed unchanged". **M**

**A9. ERP peaks cannot be confirmed (3)**
- **What and where:** the peak table is read-only and nothing is logged. `b_results.js` erp().
- **Spec source:** 3.4 Clinician in control; table 3.3 ERP peak values.
- **Proposal:** draggable peak markers within their windows, "Confirm peaks" per trial type, a log line, a report sentence, and a warning before issue. **M**

**A10. Support view can act, and its actions are logged as the clinician (2)**
- **What and where:** support staff can confirm, edit and issue. Free text stays visible. Opened cases are not logged.
- **Spec source:** 3.6 Who sees what; Q9 proposal (a).
- **Proposal:** make support view read-only and hide free text. Log actions under "Company support". Derive the banner end time from the 48 h grant. **S**

## C. Should fix (quality, consistency)

**C1. Rounding differs (3).** The same value prints as z = -2.0 in the PDF and -2.1 in the prototype. Use one half-up rounding function for both pipelines and add a parity check. **S**

**C2. Forbidden-word checks too weak (3).** The German check missed all probe words. Prototype strings are not checked. The clinic claim check passes "abnormal", "Befund" and "FDA". Build the lists from intended-use section 4 and present clinic texts as I6 option (b), company review. **M**

**C3. No lock after issue (1).** Segment and cleaning changes after issue silently reset the stage. Make those steps read-only after issue. Starting a correction reopens them, and upstream changes reopen the later confirmations. **M**

**C4. Mandatory blocks (2).** The issuer line can disappear with the conclusion block, the summary can be removed, and comments can be switched off. Add a required "Issued by" block with "replaces vN" and the reason, make the summary required, and remove the comments switch. **S**

**C5. Choice statements outside the sentence library (1).** Segment, marker and peak statements are interface strings without ids. Move them into `textlib` with ids such as Q-SEG-MAN-1 and MK-MOVED-1. **S**
