# V4 mockup

A disposable mockup of V4 for discussion with the clinical lead and the CTO.

- **Synthetic data only.** Not a medical device, not validated, not product code.
- **Specification:** repository `PershinIlya/v4` and the shared document "V4: key specification
  and open questions". Where they are silent, the choice is listed below.

| M1 | Synthetic patient: a girl of 10 years, case code MOCK-042, display name "Mock Patient 042", birth date 1 January 2016; current measurement 15 September 2026, earlier measurement 12 March 2026, and an earliest measurement of 23 September 2025 uploaded later (M55); eyes open, eyes closed and VCPT in each. Besides random deviations the child has four stable deviations in four analysis blocks, chosen so that together they form no published pattern of any condition: lower relative theta power at posterior sites (P3, Pz, P4, O1, O2), a higher SMR asymmetry index at C3-C4 (more SMR at C4), higher beta1 coherence among T3, C3, T5, P3 and O1 (7 pairs), and a larger (more negative) occipital N1 at O1 and O2 in every trial type (scored in Go), which replaces the earlier P1 latency delay (review C10); targets and shapes in M66. Frontal theta, the theta/beta ratio, P1, the NoGo N2 and P3, the CNV and the VCPT results stay within ±1.5 z. Mixed on purpose: between any two measurements two deviations move towards the norm and two away, so the series does not read as a story. The seeds are picked so that the acceptance checks of M67 pass (no scalp-wide shift of any band, spectra falling above 20 Hz, eyes closed alpha reactivity and scalp distribution as in the norm, total power about the same between measurements, so no gain change is suggested, one chance mark at a mid-range value). Values beyond 2 z are fewer than chance alone gives (M67). |
| M2 | Norms are invented ("Mock norms 2026.1"): a synthetic norm sample of 400 children drawn from the same model gives the mean and SD of every measure at the patient's age (pointwise for spectra and ERP waveforms), so z, percentile and values agree and a value without a stable deviation has a z with unit variance. This stands in for the one continuous model of age and sex per measure (GAMLSS) of the shared document (3.2 Normative model, Working; needs the clinical lead's agreement, section 1); the generator draws at the patient's age and ignores sex, although MET-NORM-1 names a model of age and sex (MC7 of 30 October). Spectra (average reference, uV^2/Hz): 1/f background, temporal sites lower, fronto-central theta shoulder, an alpha peak near 9.2 Hz at 10 years at every site, largest at posterior sites and several times larger with the eyes closed; with the eyes closed absolute alpha band power at posterior sites (P3, Pz, P4, O1, O2) is about 5 times the frontal value in the norm (about 2.4 times with the eyes open). Between-subject spread: alpha peak (SD 0.65 Hz), alpha amplitude, a broadband part per channel (SD 0.28 log10 units) and a part that differs between frequencies (about 0.13), so absolute band power has an SD of about 0.3 log10 units and relative power about 0.07 to 0.17. Of the spectral random variance 60 % is common to all sites (one child's power is higher or lower at every electrode alike) and 40 % is smooth across the scalp, so a child keeps the norm's scalp distribution. The alpha peak is read off the posterior mean spectrum after taking off the 1/f trend. Coherence norms fall with distance; coherence z is computed on Fisher's scale, atanh of the square root of coherence. Random deviations are smooth across the scalp (and across frequency); of the broadband part of the spectra 85 % of the variance is the child's own and shared by both measurements (a child's overall power changes little in six months), of all other random deviations 55 %; the rest differs per recording, with the recording's broadband part shared by eyes open and eyes closed of one measurement. |

| M4 | Frontal alpha asymmetry index = ln(F4 alpha) minus ln(F3 alpha); asymmetry map = (right minus left) / (right plus left) per homologous pair. |
| M5 | VCPT: 100 trials each of Go, NoGo, Ignore and Novelty (legacy trial types AA, AP, PP, PH); ERPs shown from -200 to 800 ms around the second picture, which follows the cue by 1100 ms (legacy picture times 300 and 1400 ms after the trial start, audit `14b`); baseline before the cue, so the CNV after an animal cue shows as a negativity before 0; peaks P1 (70 to 170 ms) and N1 (140 to 250 ms) at O1 and O2 (Go), N2 at Fz and Cz (NoGo), P3 at Pz (Go) and at Cz and Fz (NoGo), P3a at Cz (Novelty, beyond table 3.3, M40), CNV at Cz (Go, mean of -200 to 0 ms). Waveforms are average-referenced and child-sized; the spread of amplitudes and latencies between children and recordings is given in M68 (review C11; the earlier 20 % and 2 ms plus 5 % are superseded); residual noise grows as fewer trials are accepted. |
| M6 | Sentences follow the boolean "marked" (M33), not a threshold on \|z\|: values of analyses with many values (band power, maps, asymmetry, coherence, ERP peaks) get their own sentence only when marked.
